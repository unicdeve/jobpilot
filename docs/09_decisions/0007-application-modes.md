# ADR-0007: Three Application Modes

## Status

Superseded by ADR-0014 (`0014-scouts-and-no-unattended-submission.md`).

Assist and Review stand. Autopilot does not: unattended work moved to a
Scout, and automatic submission was declined. This record is kept for what
it says about why three modes looked right at the time.

## Decision

Support Assist, Review, and Autopilot. Review is the default.

## Rationale

Progressive automation while preserving human control over consequential actions.
